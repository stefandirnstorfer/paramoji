What are paramojis

A paramoji is a parametric icon that represents facial expressions on continuous scales. Unlike enumerated emojis, its expression is rendered from parameters chosen freely on continuous axes, so it can visualize measured, detected or computationally generated emotional data with arbitrary nuance. The resulting icons are expressive, exaggerated and playful, and their emotional content is easily recognized by humans.
